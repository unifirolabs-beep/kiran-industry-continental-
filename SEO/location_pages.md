## 4. Local Landing Page SEO Implementation Checklist
*When publishing these location pages on the website, ensure the following are integrated:*

1.  **Unique Title Tag**: e.g., *Tile Adhesive Manufacturer in Karnataka | KI'S Continental*
2.  **Unique Meta Description**: e.g., *Kiran Industries is a premier tile adhesive manufacturer in Karnataka. We supply premium polymer-modified adhesives, ready plaster, and block joint mortar statewide.*
3.  **Local Schema Markup**: Add a localized JSON-LD schema showing regional address/contact mappings.
4.  **Local Dealer Call to Action**: A button linking to the *Dealers Page* or *Contact Page* encouraging local hardware stores and distributors to register.
5.  **Factory Image**: Show photos of the automated Manvi manufacturing plant to demonstrate real regional capacity.
