## 2. Product-Specific FAQs

### Q4: Which Continental adhesive should I use for fixing vitrified tiles?
**A**: For vitrified tiles on interior floors and walls, **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 2** (C2 Grade) is highly recommended. It features anti-slip technology and enhanced bond strength. For large format vitrified tiles (above 800x800 mm) on walls or exterior surfaces, **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 3** (C2TE) or **Type 4** (C2S1) is required.

### Q5: What is S1 flexibility rating in Continental Type 4 adhesive?
**A**: The S1 classification (under EN 12004) indicates a "deformable" cement-based adhesive that can deflect at least 2.5 mm under stress without losing its bond. This flexibility is vital for stone cladding, terraces, balconies, and underfloor heating layouts where structural movement, thermal expansion, or vibration occurs.

### Q6: Can I use Continental adhesives for tile-on-tile applications?
**A**: Yes. **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 2** and **Type 3** are formulated with advanced polymer resins that enable high-strength bonding over smooth, non-porous surfaces like existing ceramic or vitrified tiles, provided the surface is cleaned and structurally sound.

### Q7: What is the coverage of Continental Ready Plaster?
**A**: **Continental Ready Plaster** covers approximately 16–18 sq.ft per 40 kg bag at a uniform application thickness of 10 mm. The actual coverage depends on the flatness of the masonry blockwork or brick substrate.

### Q8: How thick should the block joint mortar be applied?
**A**: **Continental Block Joint Mortar** is engineered as a thin-bed jointing system. It should be applied at a thickness of **3 mm to 5 mm**. This replaces the traditional 15 mm sand-cement mortar joints, reducing overall building dead load and mortar consumption by up to 75%.
