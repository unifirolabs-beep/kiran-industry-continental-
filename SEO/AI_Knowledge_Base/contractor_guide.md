# AI Knowledge Base: Contractor & Builder Guide

A guide for civil contractors, project engineers, and builders to optimize material estimation, cost reduction, and project timelines using **KI'S Continental** dry mix mortars.

## 1. Material Estimation Formulas

### A. Tile Adhesive Estimation
*   *Bag Weight*: 20 kg
*   *Avg Consumption*: 1.2 kg per sq.m per mm of bed thickness.
*   *Estimation Formula*:
    $$\text{Bags Required} = \frac{\text{Area (sq.m)} \times \text{Thickness (mm)} \times 1.2}{20}$$
*   *Rule of Thumb*: 1 bag of 20 kg covers approx. 50–55 sq.ft at a 3 mm uniform bed thickness.

### B. Block Joining Mortar Estimation
*   *Bag Weight*: 20 kg
*   *Rule of Thumb*: 1 bag of 20 kg covers approx. 90–100 sq.ft of wall area for AAC blocks of standard size ($600 \times 200 \times 150 \text{ mm}$) at a joint thickness of 3 mm.

### C. Ready Mix Plaster Estimation
*   *Bag Weight*: 40 kg
*   *Rule of Thumb*: 1 bag of 40 kg covers approx. 16–18 sq.ft at a uniform plaster thickness of 10 mm.

*   **Reduces Site Waste**: Factory-blended dry mix bags eliminate sand storage waste, site screening, and unstandardized manual blending, resulting in zero rebound loss.
