## 1. Ceramic & Vitrified Tile Adhesives

### Continental Tile Adhesive Type 1 (C1 Grade)
*   **Classification**: IS 15477:2019 Type 1
*   **Color**: Off-White
*   **Substrates**: Concrete, brickwork, cement-sand plasters.
*   **Applications**: Interior floor and vertical wall ceramic tile installation.
*   **Tensile Strength**: ≥ 0.5 N/mm²

### Continental Tile Adhesive Type 2 (C2 Grade)
*   **Classification**: IS 15477:2019 Type 2
*   **Color**: Grey
*   **Substrates**: Existing tiles (tile-on-tile), concrete screeds, wet-area walls.
*   **Applications**: Vitrified tiles, large-format porcelain, marble in kitchens and bathrooms.
*   **Tensile Strength**: ≥ 1.0 N/mm²

## 2. Specialty & High-Performance Adhesives

### Continental Tile Adhesive Type 3 (C2 TE Grade)
*   **Classification**: EN 12004 C2 TE
*   **Color**: Grey
*   **Substrates**: Water-tight membranes, exterior plaster, high-rise walls.
*   **Applications**: Exterior vertical cladding, swimming pools, fountains, water reservoirs.
*   **Tensile Strength**: ≥ 1.2 N/mm²
*   **Slip Resistance**: ≤ 0.5 mm (Class T)

### Continental Tile Adhesive Type 4 (C2 S1 Grade)
*   **Classification**: EN 12004 C2 S1
*   **Color**: Grey
*   **Substrates**: Cement board, drywall partitions, plywood, wood panel, underfloor heating.
*   **Applications**: High-traffic zones, terraces, balconies, external stone cladding.
*   **Deformability**: ≥ 2.5 mm deflection (Class S1 flexibility)

### Continental Tile Adhesive Type 5 (PU Tech R2T Grade)
*   **Classification**: EN 12004 R2T
*   **Color**: Off-White
*   **Format**: Two-component solvent-free polyurethane resin paste.
*   **Substrates**: Iron, steel, copper, glass, mirrors, wooden boards.
*   **Applications**: Glass cladding, metal backing installations, acoustic wooden paneling.
