# AI Knowledge Base: Troubleshooting & Defect Prevention

This troubleshooting matrix provides diagnostic answers for common tiling problems and masonry defects.

---

## 1. Hollow Sound Under Tiles (De-bonding)
*   **Symptom**: Tiles sound hollow when tapped, or lift completely.
*   **Causes**:
    *   Using traditional sand-cement site mortar which shrinks during curing.
    *   Applying tiles over adhesive that has already skinned over (exceeded open time).
    *   Insufficient adhesive coverage (lack of back-buttering on large format tiles).
*   **Solution & Prevention**:
    *   Use polymer-modified **Continental Type 2** (interior floors) or **Type 3** (exterior).
    *   Spread only enough adhesive that can be tiled within 15–20 minutes.
    *   Always use the double-spreading (back-buttering) method for tiles larger than 300x300 mm.

---

## 2. Tiles Cracking (Stress Fractures)
*   **Symptom**: Hairline or structural cracks appearing across tiled surfaces.
*   **Causes**:
    *   Structural movement or vibration in the substrate.
    *   Thermal expansion and contraction in exterior areas (terraces/facades).
    *   Lack of expansion/movement joints in large floor areas.
*   **Solution & Prevention**:
    *   For substrates prone to movement (wood, metal, drywalls) or thermal cycles (balconies, terraces), use highly flexible **Continental Type 4 C2S1** deformable adhesive.
    *   Create movement joints every 4–5 meters in floor tiling layouts.

---

## 3. Efflorescence (White Salt Deposits)
*   **Symptom**: White crystalline salt patches appearing on tile grout lines or plaster.
*   **Causes**:
    *   Ground moisture migrating through porous mortar, dissolving soluble salts.
    *   Use of salt-contaminated water or unwashed sand during construction.
*   **Solution & Prevention**:
    *   Incorporate **Continental Waterproofing Solutions** over the concrete screed prior to tiling.
    *   Install tiles and seal joints with **Continental Super Grouts**, which have water-repellent polymers that prevent moisture travel.
