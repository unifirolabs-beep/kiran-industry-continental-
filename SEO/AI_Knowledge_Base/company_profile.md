## 3. Product Range Overview
Kiran Industries manufactures factory-blended dry mix cementitious and polyurethane products:
1.  **Tile Adhesives**: Polymer-modified adhesives classified from standard interior bonds to S1-deformable and PU technology (Type-1, Type-2, Type-3, Type-4, Type-5).
2.  **Super Grouts**: CG2-grade polymer-modified stain-resistant anti-fungal tile joint fillers.
3.  **Ready Mix Plaster**: Factory-controlled sand-cement wall plaster.
4.  **Block Joining Mortar**: Thin-bed high-bond masonry jointing adhesive.
5.  **Wall Putty**: White cement and acrylic-modified base coat for paint preparation.
6.  **Waterproofing Solutions**: Seamless positive and negative pressure cementitious membrane barriers.
