## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What makes KI'S Continental a premium tile adhesive brand in India?
**A**: KI'S Continental uses premium grade OPC cement, pure processed quartz sand, and high-performance polymers imported from global market leaders. The factory's fully automated 20 TPH plant ensures that every bag manufactured matches strict IS and EN standards, eliminating the batch-to-batch variations common in manual plants.

### Q2: Which tile adhesive is best for large-format vitrified tiles in living rooms?
**A**: For standard vitrified tiles on floors, **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 2** (C2 Grade) is highly recommended. If the tiles are exceptionally large (greater than 800x800 mm) or installed on walls, **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 3** or **Type 4** should be used to accommodate structural weight and micro-movements.

### Q3: Why should I use Block Joining Mortar instead of traditional sand-cement mortar?
**A**: **Continental Block Joint Mortar** allows for a thin-bed joint thickness of 3–5 mm, compared to 15 mm for sand-cement. This reduces mortar weight, material consumption, and curing time. Additionally, block joint mortar is shrinkage-resistant, which prevents micro-cracks along masonry joints.

### Q4: Can I apply tile adhesive directly over an existing tiled floor?
**A**: Yes, using **Continental Tile Adhesive Type 2** or **Type 3**. The substrate must be structurally sound and scrubbed clean. The high polymer content of our Type 2 and Type 3 adhesives ensures a strong tile-on-tile bond.

### Q5: What is the S1 rating in Continental Type 4 adhesive?
**A**: The S1 rating denotes "deformability" under the European EN 12004 standard. It guarantees that the cured adhesive can deflect at least 2.5 mm without cracking or losing adhesion. This is critical for external stone cladding, balconies, and structures exposed to heavy vibration or extreme thermal expansion.
