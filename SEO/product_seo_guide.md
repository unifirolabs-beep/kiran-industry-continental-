# Product SEO & AI Optimization Requirements: KI'S Continental

To ensure that each product page is crawled, understood, and ranked by Google and featured in AI agent answers, every product must present structured technical data in a clear layout. Below are the content requirements, technical parameters, and structured content maps for the KI'S Continental product range.

---

## 1. Structured Content Mapping (Per Product)

For every product listing on the website, ensure the following fields are present in the HTML/JSX. AI search bots crawl for these exact entities:

1.  **Product Name**: e.g., *Continental Tile Adhesive Type 1*
2.  **Product Type**: e.g., *Cementitious Tile Adhesive*
3.  **Grade/Classification**: e.g., *IS 15477:2019 Type 1 (C1 Grade)*
4.  **Applications**: Bulleted list of compatible substrates and areas.
5.  **Key Features**: Anti-slump, water-resistant, flexible, etc.
6.  **Advantages**: e.g., *No pre-soaking of tiles, saves labour, minimizes material wastage.*
7.  **Technical Specifications Table**: Tensile strength, pot life, open time.
8.  **Mixing Ratio**: Water demand per bag.
9.  **Coverage Calculator**: Estimated coverage based on notch size and bed thickness.
10. **Packaging**: e.g., *20 kg / 40 kg moisture-proof laminated bags.*
11. **Storage & Shelf Life**: e.g., *Store in a cool, dry place. Shelf life: 12 months.*
12. **Downloads Section**: Clickable links to PDF Technical Data Sheets (TDS) and Safety Data Sheets (SDS).
13. **FAQ Accordion**: 2-3 questions specific to that product.

## 2. Technical Product Parameter Master

### Tile Adhesive Type-1 (C1 Grade)
*   **Mixing Ratio**: 4.4 to 4.8 Litres of water per 20 kg bag.
*   **Coverage**: 50–55 sq.ft per 20 kg bag (at 3 mm thickness).
*   **Storage**: 12 months shelf life from date of manufacture.
*   **Standards**: IS 15477 Type 1.

*   **Standards**: IS 15477 Type 2.

*   **Standards**: EN 12004 C2 TE.

*   **Mixing Ratio**: 6.5 to 7.2 Litres of water per 40 kg bag.
*   **Coverage**: 16–18 sq.ft per 40 kg bag (at 10 mm thickness).
*   **Storage**: 6 months shelf life from date of manufacture.
*   **Standards**: IS 2250.

### Super Grouts (CG2 Grade Grout)
*   **Mixing Ratio**: 280 to 300 ml of water (or grout admix) per 1 kg of powder.
*   **Coverage**: Varies based on joint width and tile dimensions.
*   **Storage**: 24 months shelf life.
*   **Standards**: EN 13888 CG2WA.

## 3. Image SEO Guidelines
*Search engine image crawlers index photos based on contextual data. Implement the following checklist:*

*   **File Naming**: Never upload files named `image_1.jpg` or `product.png`. Use descriptive hyphens:
    *   *Correct*: `kis-continental-type1-ceramic-tile-adhesive.webp`
    *   *Correct*: `kiran-industries-automated-manufacturing-plant-manvi.webp`
*   **Format**: Use **WebP** for all product photos to minimize page load times and satisfy Core Web Vitals.
*   **ALT Text**: Every product image must contain descriptive ALT tags detailing the brand name and product use:
    *   *Correct*: `<img src="..." alt="KI'S Continental Type-1 Tile Adhesive for Ceramic Floor and Wall Tiles" />`
*   **Captions**: Provide visible descriptive text below the image where appropriate.
