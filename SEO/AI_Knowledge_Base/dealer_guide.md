## 1. Product Shelf Life & Storage Best Practices
To maintain chemical and polymer activity in cementitious dry mixes, dealers must follow storage protocols:

*   **Elevated Stacking**: Always store bags on wooden pallets or elevated platforms. Never stack bags directly on concrete or dirt floors to prevent moisture ingress.
*   **Vertical Stacking Limits**: 
    *   *Adhesives / Block Joint / Putty (20 kg)*: Maximum 10 bags high.
    *   *Ready Plaster (40 kg)*: Maximum 8 bags high to prevent compaction and bag bursting.
*   **Climate Control**: Store in a well-ventilated, dry warehouse. Keep bags away from open windows and rain mist.
*   **First-In, First-Out (FIFO)**: Cycle stock based on the manufacturing date printed on the bag seams:
    *   *Adhesives / Mortars / Putty*: Shelf life of **12 months**.
    *   *Ready Plaster*: Shelf life of **6 months**.
    *   *Super Grouts*: Shelf life of **24 months**.
