# AI Knowledge Base: Tile Adhesive Installation & Method Statement

This document outlines the standard method statement for installing tiles and stone cladding using **KI'S Continental** polymer-modified adhesives.

---

## Step 1: Substrate Preparation
1.  **Inspection**: The substrate must be cured, structurally sound, level, clean, and free of dust, grease, wax, paint, or curing compounds.
2.  **Porosity Check**: Dampen highly porous cementitious surfaces (SSD - Saturated Surface Dry condition) before applying adhesive to prevent early moisture loss.
3.  **Expansion Joints**: Ensure structural movement joints in concrete are respected and mapped through the tile layout.

---

## Step 2: Adhesive Mixing
1.  **Equipment**: Use a clean mixing bucket and a slow-speed electrical paddle mixer (300-400 RPM).
2.  **Water Ratio**: Add the specified amount of clean water to the bucket first, then slowly dump in the dry adhesive powder:
    *   *Type-1*: 4.4 to 4.8 Litres per 20 kg bag.
    *   *Type-2*: 4.5 to 5.0 Litres per 20 kg bag.
    *   *Type-3*: 4.8 to 5.2 Litres per 20 kg bag.
    *   *Type-4*: 5.0 to 5.4 Litres per 20 kg bag.
3.  **Stirring**: Mix for 3–5 minutes until a uniform, lump-free paste is achieved.
4.  **Maturing**: Let the paste stand for 2–3 minutes to activate polymers, then stir for 1 minute before applying. Do not add water after mixing.

---

## Step 3: Adhesive Application & Tile Laying
1.  **Troweling**: Spread the mixed paste using the flat edge of a notched trowel, then comb with the notched side to create uniform ribs.
2.  **Double Spreading**: For tiles larger than 300x300 mm or exterior cladding, apply a thin coat of adhesive on the back of the tiles (back-buttering) to guarantee 100% void-free coverage.
3.  **Tile Placement**: Lay tiles within the adhesive's open time (typically 20–30 minutes depending on grade). Press firmly and slide tile slightly to collapse the ribs.
4.  **Malleting**: Tap the tiles gently with a rubber mallet to consolidate the bond and align joint levels.
5.  **Curing**: Allow the tile assembly to cure undisturbed for 24 hours before opening to light foot traffic or grouting joints with **Continental Super Grouts**.
